# Speaking — Banco de frases útiles

## Elegir un tema
I'd like to talk about this topic (because…) · I would choose this one because it's easier.

## Dar opiniones
I think it's very interesting · In my opinion, it's important · I think it depends on the situation · I think it's better to… · I think it's more stressful than… · I'm not sure, but I think…

## Explicar razones
Because… · The main reason is that… · Because everything is new · Because panic makes things worse · Because caffeine keeps you awake.

## Dar ejemplos y experiencias
For example… · For example, in my experience… · I remember my first job · On my first day… · If I were in that situation… · When I started my last job…

## Invitar al compañero
What do you think (about that)? · What about you? · How about you? · Do you agree (with me)? · Would you do the same? · Would you feel the same? · What would you do (in my situation)? · Do you feel the same way?

## Preguntas de seguimiento
Why would you feel sad/embarrassed? · How would you do that? · How do you deal with that? · Does it happen to you? · Have you ever felt the same way? · Have you ever felt nervous on your first day at work? · Why do you think that?

## Estar de acuerdo
I agree (with you / with that) · I totally agree · Yes, absolutely · That's true · That's a good point · I'd do the same.

## Estar en desacuerdo
I see your point, but… · That's a good point, but I disagree · I'm not sure about that · On the other hand… · I understand, but…

## Comparar
I think it's more stressful than… · I think it's better than… · Both situations can be exciting · On the other hand… · …is different from…

## Ganar tiempo (evitar bloqueos)
Let me think for a second · That's a difficult question · I'm not sure, but I think… · Let me see… · I guess… · I mean… · Actually…

## ⭐ Las 15 frases memorizadas
**Grupo 1 — Elegir tema y opinar**
1. I'd like to talk about this topic.
2. I think it's very interesting.
3. In my opinion, it's important.
4. I would choose this one because it's easier.

**Grupo 2 — Explicar y ejemplificar**
5. For example, in my experience…
6. I would try to stay calm.
7. I think it depends on the situation.
8. The main reason is that…

**Grupo 3 — Interactuar**
9. What do you think about that?
10. Do you agree with me?
11. That's a good point, but I disagree.
12. I agree with you because…

**Grupo 4 — Evitar bloqueos**
13. Let me think for a second.
14. That's a difficult question.
15. I'm not sure, but I think…

### Errores detectados al memorizar
- I like → **I'd like** (me gusta vs me gustaría)
- I would choice → **I would choose** (choose = verbo / choice = sustantivo)
- That's good point → **That's a good point**
- That's difficult question → **That's a difficult question**
- Let me think, but for a second → **Let me think for a second**
