# Paper 3 — Sleep Problems
**"How do these things prevent people from getting enough sleep?"** → presente simple, causas, consecuencias, ejemplos.

Subtópicos: consuming caffeine · smoking · jet lag · daytime napping · noisy bedrooms.

## Consuming caffeine
- I'd like to talk about caffeine because many people drink coffee every day.
- Because caffeine keeps you awake. For example, if I drink coffee late at night, I can't sleep well.
- I think drinking coffee in the morning is different from drinking it at night. Do you usually drink coffee in the evening?
- ⚠️ difficult to feel sleep → **difficult to fall asleep**; mantené el mismo sujeto: *if I drink coffee late at night, I can't sleep well*.

## Smoking
- I think smoking can affect your sleep because nicotine keeps you awake.
- For example, my heart beats faster and I can't relax.
- *That's good for your health.*

## Jet lag
- Jet lag can make you feel tired during the day. For example, I wake up at 3 a.m.
- *How do you deal with that?* → I try to sleep earlier and drink water.
- ⚠️ cuidado **can vs can't** (cambia todo el sentido); verificar en oral, no por transcripción.

## Daytime napping
- Long naps can make it hard to sleep at night.
- I agree that long naps can affect your sleep. On the other hand, I think short naps can be good for you.

## Noisy bedrooms
- Do you think noise affects sleep? → Yes, definitely.
- For example, if my neighbours make a lot of noise, I find it difficult to fall asleep. I think a quiet bedroom is very important.
- ⚠️ neighbourhood (barrio) ≠ **neighbours** (vecinos).
