# Paper 2 — Feelings and Situations
**"How would you feel in these situations?"**

Subtópicos principales: moving to a new home · financial problems · starting a new job · first date · getting married.
Complementarios (práctica extra): failing an exam · being criticized at work · not getting a job.

## Moving to a new home
- Moving to a new home, I would feel excited but nervous because everything is new.
- *What about you? Would you feel the same?* → *Yes, but I'd also feel sad.* → *Why would you feel sad?*
> **Why WOULD you feel sad?** (situación imaginada) vs **Why DO you feel sad?** (sentimiento real presente).

## Financial problems
- Having financial problems, I would feel stressed and worried because money is important.
- *Why would you feel embarrassed?*
- Vocab: stressed · worried · embarrassed · financial problems.

## Starting a new job (el más desarrollado)
- I would choose starting a new job because it's something important in our lives.
- I think I would feel excited but also nervous because everything would be new: new people, new responsibilities and a new workplace.
- For example, on my first day, I would try to make a good impression.
- I'm a little shy when I meet new people.
- Have you ever felt nervous on your first day at work?
- I think the first days are difficult, but after some time, you get used to the new job.
- I think starting a new job is more stressful because you have to learn many new things, but both situations can be exciting.

### ⚠️ Correcciones clave
- I will choose → **I would choose** · will be new → **would be new**
- New responsibility → **responsibilities** · a good impressions → **a good impression**
- In your first day → **On your first day** · after some times → **after some time**
- Both situation → **Both situations**

## Failing an exam (extra)
- If I failed an exam, I would feel disappointed. I would study more next time.
- ⚠️ *Maybe I take a breakfast* → **Maybe I'd take a break first.** (take a break = descansar / have breakfast = desayunar)

## Being criticized at work (extra)
- If I were criticized at work, I would feel frustrated. I would ask for feedback.

## Not getting a job (extra)
- If I didn't get the job, I would feel sad but hopeful. I would apply again.

> **first date** y **getting married**: subtópicos pendientes de profundizar.
