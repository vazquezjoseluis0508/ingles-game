# Speaking — Senior 5 Test

## Paper 1 — What would you do if you got lost in these places? (second conditional)
areas of high crime · Amazon rainforest · Everest · Atacama Desert · city in a foreign country
> *If I got lost in the Amazon rainforest, I would try to stay calm and find a safe place.*

## Paper 2 — How would you feel in these situations?
moving to a new home · financial problems · first date · starting a new job · getting married
> *I would feel excited but nervous.* · *Financial problems would make me stressed and worried.*

## Paper 3 — How do these things prevent people from getting enough sleep?
consuming caffeine · smoking · jet lag · daytime napping · noisy bedrooms

## Objetivo principal: INTERACCIÓN
**Evitar:** hablar demasiado · monopolizar · ignorar al compañero.
**Conviene:** 1) opinión → 2) explicar → 3) ejemplo → 4) preguntar al compañero → 5) reaccionar.
Estructura útil: *I think… because… For example… What do you think?*
