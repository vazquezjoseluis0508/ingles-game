# Paper 1 — Getting Lost
**"What would you do if you got lost in these places?"** → *second conditional:* If + past simple, would + base verb.

Subtópicos: Amazon rainforest · Everest · Atacama Desert · foreign city · area of high crime.

## Amazon rainforest
- If I got lost in the Amazon rainforest, I would feel really scared.
- First, I would try to stay calm because panic makes things worse.
- Then, I would look for a safe place and try to call for help.
- I would avoid dangerous animals and stay away from the river.
- *What about you? Would you do the same?* · *That's a good point, but rivers can be dangerous too.*
- If my phone didn't work, I would try to find a safe place to spend the night.

## Near Everest
- If I got lost near Everest, I would feel cold and nervous.
- First, I would look for shelter from the wind.
- Vocab: shelter (refugio) · wind (viento) · shout for help (gritar pidiendo ayuda).

## Atacama Desert
- If I got lost in the Atacama Desert, I would feel thirsty and worried.
- First, I would look for shade and water.
- *I see your point, but I think I would look for help.*
- Vocab: thirsty (sediento) · shade /ʃeɪd/ (sombra) · stay still (quedarse quieto) · save energy.

## Foreign city
- If I got lost in a foreign city, I would try to use Google Maps because it's quick and easy.
- If my phone was dead, I would try to find someone to ask for help.
- Versión natural: *If I got lost in a foreign city, I would try to stay calm and find a public place with lots of people.*
- Vocab: foreign city · ask for help · my phone is dead · find my way back.

## Area of high crime
- If I got lost in an area of high crime, I would feel very nervous and stay in a public place.
- *That's true. I'd do the same.*

### ⚠️ Correcciones clave Paper 1

- Because find a river → **Because finding a river**
- It's very dangerous place → **It's a very dangerous place**
