# Vocabulary — Media and Journalism

## Headline language (palabras cortas de titulares)
| Word | Español | Example |
|---|---|---|
| back | apoyar | Prime Minister backs proposal. |
| axe | eliminar / recortar | Thousands of jobs axed. |
| hit | afectar negativamente | Stock market hit by oil fears. |
| bid | intento | Astronaut bids to be first man on Mars. |
| clash | conflicto / enfrentamiento | Ministers clash over tax proposal. |
| vow | prometer firmemente | Bayern boss vows to avenge defeat. |
| quiz | interrogar | Police quiz witness. |
| row | discusión / disputa | Actress rows with co-star. |
