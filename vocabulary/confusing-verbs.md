# Vocabulary — Confusing Verbs

| Pair | Uso | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **advise** | aconsejar | The doctor advised me to rest. |
| **warn** | advertir sobre un peligro | They warned us about the storm. |
| **discuss** | hablar/debatir un tema | We discussed the problem. |
| **argue** | discutir / pelear verbalmente | They argued about money. |
| **notice** | notar por los sentidos | I noticed a strange noise. |
| **realize** | darse cuenta / comprender | I realized I was late. |
| **avoid** | evitar hacer/encontrarse | I avoid driving at night. |
| **prevent** | impedir que algo suceda | This can prevent accidents. |
| **lend** | prestar | Can you lend me some money? |
| **borrow** | pedir prestado | Can I borrow your pen? |
| **mind** | molestar | Do you mind if I open the window? |
| **matter** | importar | It doesn't matter. |
| **remember** | recordar | I remember her name. |
| **remind** | recordarle algo a alguien | Remind me to call him. |
| **expect** | esperar que algo ocurra | I expect him to arrive soon. |
| **wait** | esperar físicamente | I'm waiting for the bus. |
| **wish** | desear algo irreal/difícil | I wish I had more time. |
| **hope** | esperar algo posible | I hope you pass the exam. |

## Patrones importantes
- **beat + opponent** → *River beat Boca.* · **win + competition/game/prize** → *They won the match.*
- **refuse to do** → *He refused to answer.* · **deny doing** → *He denied stealing the money.*
- **raise + object** (levantar algo) → *Raise your hand.* · **rise** (subir solo) → *Prices are rising.*
- **lay + object** (colocar algo) → *Lay the book on the table.* · **lie** (acostarse) → *He was lying on the bed.*
