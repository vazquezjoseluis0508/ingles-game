# Vocabulary — Feelings (-ed / -ing)

- **-ed** = cómo se siente una persona → *I'm bored.* · *I'm excited.*
- **-ing** = lo que provoca ese sentimiento → *The film is boring.* · *The trip is exciting.*

## Situaciones practicadas
- moving to a new home → *I feel excited but a little nervous.*
- financial problems → *I feel worried and stressed.*
- starting a new job → *I feel nervous but motivated.*
- first date
- getting married
