## Criminals
blackmailer · briber · burglar · drug dealer · fraudster · hacker · hijacker · kidnapper · mugger · murderer · rapist · robber · smuggler · stalker · terrorist · thief · vandal

> **stalk → stalker**: *to stalk someone* = acosar · *a stalker* = acosador

## ⚠️ rob vs steal
- **rob + person/place** → *They robbed the bank.* / *Someone robbed him.*
- **steal + object** → *They stole my phone.* / *Someone stole the money.*
- ❌ *They stole the bank.*  ❌ *They robbed my phone.*
