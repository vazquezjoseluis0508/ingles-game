# Grammar — Modal Deduction

## Present
- **must** = casi seguro → *He must be tired.*
- **might / may / could** = posible → *She might be at home.*
- **can't** = casi imposible → *He can't be serious.*

## Past — modal + have + past participle
- **must have** (casi seguro) → *You must have been delighted.*
- **might / may / could have** (posible) → *He might have gone home.*
- **might not have** (quizá no) → *She might not have read the email.*
- **can't / couldn't have** (casi imposible) → *You can't have seen my parents. They're away.*

### Ejercicio reciente
1. must have been  2. might have gone  3. can't have seen  4. might not have heard
5. can't have moved  6. might have fallen  7. must have done  8. might not have read

## Should have / ought to have
Algo correcto que no se hizo (pasado).
- **should have + pp** → *We should have filled up at the last garage.*
- **shouldn't have + pp** → *I shouldn't have shouted at him.*
- **ought to have + pp** → *They ought to have dressed up for the wedding.*
- **ought not to have + pp** → *He ought not to have left his phone on his desk.*

## Práctica — accident photo
- She must have fallen off her bike. · She might have broken her bike.
- She may have hurt her leg. · She could have sprained her ankle.
- She can't have been wearing enough protection. · She might have hit her head.
- She should have been more careful. · She ought not to have ridden so fast.
