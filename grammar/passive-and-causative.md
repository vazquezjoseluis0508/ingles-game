# Grammar — Passive Voice & Causative Have

## 1. Passive voice
Se usa cuando importa más la acción/el resultado que quién la realiza.
**Estructura: BE + past participle** (el tiempo lo marca BE).

| Tense | Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Present simple | am/is/are + pp | The door is locked. |
| Past simple | was/were + pp | The burglar was caught. |
| Present continuous | am/is/are being + pp | My house is being painted. |
| Present perfect | has/have been + pp | My phone has been stolen. |
| Future / going to | will be / is going to be + pp | The police station is going to be closed. |

### Ejercicios
- Somebody has stolen my phone. → **My phone has been stolen.**
- They are painting my house. → **My house is being painted.**
- They'll hold a meeting. → **A meeting will be held.**
- Miranda thinks someone was following her. → *Miranda thinks she was being followed.*

## 2. Causative have — have something done
**Estructura: have + object + past participle**

### A. Otra persona lo hace por nosotros
- I had my hair cut.  · We had our photo taken. · You should have your eyes tested.
- Our neighbours are having a swimming pool built.
> **I cut my hair** = yo mismo · **I had my hair cut** = otra persona.

### B. Algo malo nos sucede
- She had her iPhone stolen. · I had my bank account hacked. · We had our house burgled.

### Ejercicios
- Have you ever had your bag snatched?
- I'm having a tooth filled.
- My grandfather has his shopping delivered every Friday.
