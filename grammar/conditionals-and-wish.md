# Grammar — Conditionals & Wish

## Conditionals
- **First** (posible futuro): If + present, will + infinitive → *If we win the lottery, we will travel the world.*
- **Second** (hipotético presente/futuro): If + past, would + infinitive → *If she had enough money, she would buy a big house.* · *If he were younger, he would travel more.*
- **Third** (imposible pasado / arrepentimiento): If + past perfect, would have + pp → *She wouldn't have woken up late if she had gone to bed earlier.*

## Wish
- **wish + past simple** (situación presente) → *I wish I had more time.* · *I wish I were taller.*
- **wish + past perfect** (arrepentimiento pasado) → *I wish I had listened to your advice.*
- **wish + would** (querer que algo/alguien cambie) → *I wish he would stop interrupting me.*
