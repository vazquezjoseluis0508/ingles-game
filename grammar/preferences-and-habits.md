# Grammar — Preferences & Habits

## Would rather
### Mismo sujeto: would rather + base verb
- I'd rather stay home. · I'd rather see that film at the cinema. · I'd rather sit by the window.
### Otro sujeto: would rather + person + past simple
- I'd rather you cooked tonight. · I'd rather you didn't post photos of me.
- Q: *What would you rather do: stay in or go out?*

## Prefer
- I'd prefer to + verb → *I'd prefer to go to the cinema.*
- I'd prefer not to + verb → *I'd prefer not to go to the party.*
- Would you prefer to…?
- I'd prefer it if + subject + past → *I'd prefer it if you phoned earlier.*

## Used to / be used to / get used to
- **used to + infinitive** (hábito pasado que ya no ocurre) → *I used to watch TV three hours a day.* · *I didn't use to like horror films.*
- **be used to + noun/-ing** (estar acostumbrado) → *Soldiers are used to danger.* · *He is used to speaking in public.*
- **get used to + noun/-ing** (acostumbrarse) → *I can't get used to sleeping with the light on.* · *You will soon get used to it.*
