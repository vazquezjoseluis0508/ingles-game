# Writings — Structures

## 1. For-and-against / opinion article (~140–150 words)
- **P1** Introduction / present the topic.
- **P2** Advantages / arguments in favour.
- **P3** Disadvantages / arguments against.
- **P4** Conclusion + opinion.

### Connectors
Nowadays · Firstly · Secondly · For instance · For example · In addition · Moreover · However · Whereas · Nevertheless · Actually · On the other hand · In conclusion

### Temas preparados para examen
- Government banning social media for underage children.
- Professional gaming as a real sport.
- AI helping or ruining jobs.

### Condiciones que la profe puede pedir
- 2 feelings · 1 third conditional · 1 wish + past perfect · 1 wish + past simple or would · ~150 words
- 👉 Pediste que las estructuras obligatorias aparezcan **en negrita** para detectarlas rápido.

## 2. Portfolio / blog post
**"Conquering the Heights: How to Stay Safe in the Mountains"**
- feelings: *thrilled*, *scared stiff*
- second conditional → *If the weather changed, you would find…*

## 3. Speaking as mini-writing
Respuestas cortas, no discursos:
> *Starting a new job would make me nervous but excited because everything is new. I would also want to make a good impression. What about you?*

Corrección clave: evitar respuestas muy largas y cerrar con una pregunta al compañero.
