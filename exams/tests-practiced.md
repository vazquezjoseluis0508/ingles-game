## Listening — the polite robber
"Britain's most polite armed robber" (Stockport): says please and thank you · tall man · in his early forties · wears a mask · wears washing-up gloves · robbed at least four shops · uses a knife · victims terrified.
Estructuras: *is said to be* · *It is believed that* · *It is thought that* · *He is reported to be*.
> *Police are looking for a man who is said to be Britain's most polite armed robber.*
> *It is believed that he is a tall man in his early forties.*
