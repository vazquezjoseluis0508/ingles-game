# Mis errores frecuentes

1. **rob vs steal** → rob + persona/lugar · steal + objeto. (rob a bank/person · steal money/phone)
2. **infinitive vs gerund tras reporting verbs** → to inf: offer/promise/refuse/threaten/agree · person+to inf: advise/remind/persuade/invite/tell · -ing: suggest/deny/admit/recommend · prep+-ing: apologize for / accuse of / insist on.
3. **Past modals** → siempre modal + have + past participle. ❌ must went ✅ must have gone.
4. **must / might / can't have** → certeza: must (casi seguro) · might/could (posible) · can't (casi imposible).
5. **should have** → *should do* (ahora) vs *should have done* (pasado no hecho).
6. **Passive** → BE + past participle; el tiempo lo marca BE (is/was/is being/has been stolen).
7. **Causative have** → *I cut my hair* (yo) vs *I had my hair cut* (otra persona). have + object + participle.
8. **Would rather con otro sujeto** → *I'd rather you stayed* (past), ❌ *I'd rather you stay*.
9. **Speakings demasiado individuales** → tras 2–3 frases: *What do you think? / How about you? / Do you agree?* y reaccionar (*That's true / I agree / Maybe, but…*).
10. **Frases demasiado complejas** → idea simple + reason + example/question. Mejor simple y correcta.
11. **Confusing verbs** a repasar: rob/steal · lend/borrow · remember/remind · expect/wait · wish/hope · advise/warn · discuss/argue · notice/realize · avoid/prevent · refuse/deny · beat/win · raise/rise · lay/lie · mind/matter.
12. **Fluidez antes que perfección (speaking)** → respuesta breve → explicación → interacción.
